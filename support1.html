Frequently Asked Questions

How do I pair the two devices?

Open Baby Mode on the phone that will stay near your baby. On the other phone, open Parent Mode and tap Scan QR Code, then point the camera at the QR code shown in Baby Mode. The two devices don't need to be on the same Wi-Fi network.

Why isn't the microphone working?

Make sure BabyAlert Pro has microphone access under Settings → BabyAlert Pro → Microphone. If another app is using the microphone at the same time, cry detection may be interrupted.

Why am I not receiving alerts?

Check that notifications are enabled for the App under Settings → BabyAlert Pro → Notifications, that Do Not Disturb is off, and that both devices have an active internet connection — alerts are delivered remotely, so an internet connection is required even though pairing itself works over the local network or without it.

How does the family code work?

A Pro subscriber can generate a family code from Parent Mode and share it with one additional family member. That person enters the code under I Have a Family Code to unlock Parent Mode on their own device, free of charge, for as long as the subscription stays active.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely through the App Store. Go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone, select BabyAlert Pro, and cancel there. Uninstalling the App does not cancel the subscription.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the subscription screen in the App and tap Restore Purchases. This restores any active subscription linked to your Apple ID on the current device.
