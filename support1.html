Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use Chefino?

No. Chefino works without any account, login, or sign-up. Your profile, meal plans, and notes are stored directly on your device.

How do I change my calorie or macro targets?

Open your athlete profile in the app and adjust your goal, training window, and daily targets. Recipe portions and suggestions update automatically to match.

Can I use the app in my language?

Yes. Chefino is fully available in Turkish, English, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian, and Simplified Chinese — including all recipe content. The app follows your iPhone's language setting automatically. You can change it in iOS Settings → Chefino → Language.

How do I filter recipes for my diet?

Set your dietary preferences (vegetarian, gluten-free, lactose-free) in your profile, and recipes will be filtered accordingly. Please always check the ingredient list yourself if you have a serious allergy.

How do I restore my subscription on a new device?

Open the premium screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not inside the app. On your device go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions, select Chefino, and tap Cancel Subscription. Your premium access remains active until the end of the current billing period.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in with your Apple ID, and select the purchase you would like refunded.
