Privacy Policy

BabyAlert Pro · Last updated: May 23, 2026

BabyAlert Pro ("the App") cares about your privacy. This policy explains what data is processed when you use the App, why it's processed, and what rights you have.

1. Data We Collect

  • Microphone audio: Used only for on-device, real-time cry detection. Audio is never recorded, sent to a server, stored, or shared with third parties.
  • Local network data: The connection between the parent and baby devices is established directly over the same Wi-Fi network. No audio is transmitted to a server over the internet.
  • Subscription information: Purchases are processed through the Apple App Store and RevenueCat. An anonymous user identifier is kept to verify your subscription status. We do not collect your name, email address, or payment details.
  • Family code: The code you generate to share premium access is stored only on your device.

2. How We Use Data

  • Analyzing sound on-device to detect and alert you to crying.
  • Pairing the parent and baby devices on the local network.
  • Verifying your active subscription and unlocking premium features.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Apple App Store: Purchase and subscription management. Apple Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat: Subscription status verification. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

We do not use any advertising network, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

4. Data Retention

Audio data is never stored. Your anonymous subscription identifier is kept with RevenueCat for as long as your subscription remains active. If you delete the App, all local data on your device (family code, trial information, settings) is deleted as well.

5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The microphone only analyzes ambient sound intensity on-device; audio is never recorded or transmitted.

6. Your Rights

Under GDPR and other applicable data protection laws, you have the right to access, correct, and delete your data. You can reach us at the address below to make such a request.

7. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be announced within the App. The effective date is always shown at the top of this page.
