Privacy Policy

Chefino · Last updated: 27 August 2026

Short version: Chefino does not collect, transmit, or sell your personal data. Everything you enter — your profile, meal log, weekly plan, shopping list, and recipe notes — is stored only on your device. There is no account, no login, and no analytics or advertising tracking in this app.

1. Who we are

Chefino ("the app", "we", "us") is an iOS application that provides athlete-focused meal recipes, nutrition planning, and cooking guidance. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

If you have any question about this policy, contact us at ultrest12@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

Chefino works without an account. The information you provide is saved locally on your iPhone or iPad using Apple's standard on-device storage, and is not uploaded to us or to any server we control. This includes:

  • Your athlete profile: goal, training window, calorie and macronutrient targets, workouts per week, household size, cooking skill level, and available cooking time
  • Dietary preferences you select, such as vegetarian, gluten-free, or lactose-free
  • Your meal log, weekly meal plan, and marked training days
  • Your shopping list, pantry items, and saved recipe notes or ratings

We cannot see this information. It stays on your device, and it is included in your device backups only if you have enabled iCloud or local backups in iOS settings — those backups are controlled by Apple and by your own settings, not by us.

3. Information we do not collect

Chefino does not request, access, or collect:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any account credentials
  • Your location
  • Health or fitness data from Apple Health
  • Your contacts, photos, camera, or microphone
  • Advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across other apps or websites

The app contains no third-party advertising and no analytics or behavioural tracking software.

4. Purchases and subscriptions

Chefino offers an optional premium subscription. Payments are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store — we never see or handle your payment card details.

To determine whether your subscription is active, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription management service. RevenueCat receives a randomly generated, anonymous identifier together with purchase and device information (such as transaction receipts, subscription status, device model, and operating system version). This data is used only to validate and restore purchases. It is not linked to your name or to any personal profile.

5. How your information is used

The information stored on your device is used solely to operate the features you asked for: personalising recipe suggestions to your goals and dietary preferences, calculating portions, building your weekly plan and shopping list, and remembering the recipes you have cooked. It is never used for advertising or profiling, and it is never sold or shared.

6. Deleting your data

Because your data lives on your device, you are in full control of it:

  • You can clear individual entries — meals, plans, list items, notes — inside the app at any time.
  • Deleting the app from your device removes all data Chefino has stored locally.

To ask about data held by our subscription provider, or to request its deletion, email ultrest12@gmail.com and we will help you with the request.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the GDPR (European Union / United Kingdom) or the CCPA (California) to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, and to object to processing or lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

Since Chefino stores your information locally and does not maintain user accounts, most of these rights are exercised directly on your device. For anything relating to purchase data, contact us at ultrest12@gmail.com. We do not sell personal information, and we have never done so.

8. Children's privacy

Chefino is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can address it.

9. Health and nutrition disclaimer

Chefino provides general recipes and nutrition information for informational purposes only. It is not medical or dietary advice and is not a substitute for consultation with a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian. Always check recipe ingredients yourself if you have allergies or specific medical needs.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app changes. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

11. Contact
